About

I am a D.Sc. in Astronomy, Planetary and Researcher at the National Institute of Metrology, Quality and Technology (INMETRO), focusing on time keeping metrology, orbital dynamics of satellites, meteors, space debris. I coordinate the EXOSS Project, a Brazilian initiative for monitoring and researching meteors, and have international experience in space research projects collaborating with CAMS/NASA project since 2017.

My work spans from observing and analyzing astronomical phenomena to applying advanced space monitoring techniques. I worked for NASA on the FDL SETI (Frontier Development Lab Space Exploration and Technology Initiative) AI project in 2017, contributing knowledge in detection and analysis of space objects.

I am passionate about scientific outreach and the application of astronomy for the benefit of society, having given numerous interviews to outlets such as Galileu Magazine, G1, O Globo, Estadão, UOL, and participated in internationally recognized scientific program Europlanet.
