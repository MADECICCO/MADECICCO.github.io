Welcome

This is my personal page where I share my professional activities, academic projects, and contributions to citizen science, with a focus on the study of meteors and other atmospheric phenomena.

About Marcelo de Cicco

I am an astronomer and researcher with extensive experience in analyzing meteors, fireballs, and atmospheric phenomena. With a PhD in Planetary Sciences, I serve as the coordinator of the EXOSS Project, a citizen science initiative that brings together professionals and enthusiasts in monitoring space events.

Learn more about my journey on my LinkedIn and view my Lattes Curriculum.

Professional Experience

My professional work includes:

  • Research and monitoring of meteors and atmospheric phenomena.
  • Development of citizen science projects, integrating the scientific community and the interested public.
  • Collaboration with the National Observatory and other leading institutions.
  • Participation in interviews and media reports, with extensive coverage in national and international media.

Publications & Research

Throughout my career, I have published several articles and studies on topics such as:

  • Meteor Activity Forecasts – Studies on the conditions and forecasts for meteor events.
  • Meteor Monitoring – Reports and analyses conducted in partnership with the EXOSS Project and the National Observatory.
  • Atmospheric Phenomena Analysis – Research on space debris reentry and its environmental implications.
